# 🚀 Alternative App Stores & Direct Distribution Guide

Publishing to alternative stores allows you to reach hundreds of millions of Android users with **$0 developer fees** and **zero 30% store commission cuts**.

---

## 🏬 1. Huawei AppGallery (consumer.huawei.com/en/mobileservices/appgallery)
* **Cost**: $0 (100% Free)
* **Audience**: 580M+ Monthly Active Users on Huawei & Honor devices.
* **Steps**:
  1. Register free at [https://developer.huawei.com](https://developer.huawei.com).
  2. Go to **App Services > AppGallery Connect > My Apps**.
  3. Click **New App** -> Upload APK -> Submit description & screenshots.

---

## 🏬 2. Xiaomi GetApps (global.developer.mi.com)
* **Cost**: $0 (100% Free)
* **Audience**: 400M+ users across Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO smartphones worldwide.
* **Steps**:
  1. Register at [https://global.developer.mi.com](https://global.developer.mi.com).
  2. Click **Create App** -> Upload APK -> Set Category to **Productivity**.

---

## 🏬 3. Uptodown Developer Platform (developers.uptodown.com)
* **Cost**: $0 (100% Free)
* **Audience**: 130M+ Monthly Active Users worldwide.
* **Steps**:
  1. Sign in at [https://developers.uptodown.com](https://developers.uptodown.com).
  2. Upload your APK (`ASAR-Latest.apk`).
  3. Direct download goes live within 24 hours.

---

## 🏬 4. APKPure Developer Console (developer.apkpure.com)
* **Cost**: $0 (100% Free)
* **Audience**: 100M+ Monthly Active Users worldwide.
* **Steps**:
  1. Sign in at [https://developer.apkpure.com](https://developer.apkpure.com).
  2. Upload APK and store graphics.

---

## 🌐 5. Your Website (Direct Download via Vercel / GitHub)
* **Cost**: $0
* Users downloading directly from your landing page (`https://bproductive.vercel.app`) get the latest build immediately.
* Users can unlock Pro via **bKash / Nagad Transaction ID** or **Web License Key Redemption**, giving you **100% revenue** with **0% platform cuts**.
