# ASR — Free App Store Publishing Guide (No Fees Required)

How to publish ASR for **100% FREE** without paying Google's $25 fee on major alternative Android app stores.

---

## 🏬 1. Uptodown Publishing (developers.uptodown.com)
* **Cost**: $0 (100% Free)
* **Reach**: Over 130 Million active monthly Android users worldwide.
* **Requirements**: Closed-source private APK accepted.

### Steps:
1. Go to [https://developers.uptodown.com](https://developers.uptodown.com) and create a free developer account.
2. Click **Add New App** -> Choose **Upload APK**.
3. Fill in the listing details:
   - **App Title**: `ASR — Turn Time Into Progress`
   - **Package Name**: `com.aegisfocus.app`
   - **Category**: `Productivity / Tools`
   - **Short Description**: `Protect your attention. Block distracting apps. Build unbreakable focus.`
   - **Icon**: Upload `app/src/main/res/mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher.png` (512x512)
   - **Feature Graphic**: Upload `feature_graphic.png` (1024x500)
   - **Full Description**: Copy & paste text from `store_listing.md`.
4. Submit for review (takes 24-48 hours, usually approved immediately).

---

## 🏬 2. APKPure Developer Console (developer.apkpure.com)
* **Cost**: $0 (100% Free)
* **Reach**: Over 100 Million global Android users.

### Steps:
1. Go to [https://developer.apkpure.com/](https://developer.apkpure.com/) and register for free.
2. Click **Publish App** -> Select your signed `.apk` file.
3. Fill in metadata (same details from `store_listing.md`).
4. Save and Submit.

---

## 🏬 3. Samsung Galaxy Store (developer.samsung.com)
* **Cost**: $0 (100% Free)
* **Reach**: Pre-installed on all Samsung Galaxy smartphones and tablets worldwide.

### Steps:
1. Create a free Samsung Developer account at [https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store](https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store).
2. Go to **Seller Portal** -> **Add New Application** -> Select **Binary (.apk)**.
3. Upload your APK, feature graphics, and descriptions.
4. Set country distribution to Worldwide.

---

## 🔗 4. Direct Website Distribution (Vercel)
* Simply upload the generated `asr-release.apk` to your website or GitHub Releases and link it to your landing page's **Download APK** button.
