Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: September 2026

1. Google Play In-App Subscriptions

Subscriptions purchased via Google Play Billing follow Google Play's official refund policies. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through Google Play Store > Subscriptions. Cancellation stops future renewals while leaving your Pro access active until the end of the current billing cycle.

2. Direct Web Licenses (bKash, Nagad, Stripe)

For lifetime keys or web license codes purchased directly through our website, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if the software fails to perform as advertised on your device and our support team is unable to resolve the issue.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for a direct web license, open an inquiry on our official GitHub repository (github.com/khalidabdullahh/AegisFocus) with your transaction ID and license key.
