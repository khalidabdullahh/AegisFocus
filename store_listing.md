## 📄 Full Description (Formatted for Play Store Console)

**Take control of your digital attention. ASR is the next-generation focus and digital discipline app designed to turn distraction into real progress.**

Most app blockers rely on weak reminders that you easily swipe away. ASR is engineered with real psychological and technical discipline mechanisms: from **Strict Mode tamper protection** to **5-second mindful breathing friction**, giving you the focus structure you need to accomplish your most important goals.

---

### 🔥 KEY FEATURES:

#### 1. 🛡️ Advanced App Blocker & Focus Sessions
* **Instant Foreground Detection**: Blocks distracting apps (social media, games, short-form video) immediately upon opening.
* **Custom Profiles**: Create dedicated blocking rules for Work, Deep Study, Creative Flow, or Sleep.
* **Smart Schedules**: Automate recurring focus routines during your working hours or bedtime.

#### 2. 🔒 Unbreakable Strict Mode
* **Anti-Uninstall & Tamper Protection**: Once a Strict Mode session is activated, device administrator guards prevent bypassing, force-stopping, or deactivating Accessibility until the timer finishes.
* **Zero Cheating**: Built for individuals who need absolute accountability.

#### 3. 🧘 Mindful Pause & Adaptive Friction
* **5-Second Breathing Pause**: Interrupts mindless compulsive app opening, giving your brain space to make a conscious choice.
* **Escalating Delay**: Repeated attempts to open blocked apps progressively increase the waiting friction (5s → 10s → 20s → 30s).
* **Notification Shield**: Silences notifications from distracting apps during active sessions.

#### 4. 📊 Transparent Discipline Score & Deep Analytics
* **Discipline Score (10–100)**: A transparent, mathematical measure of your digital willpower based on consistency, completed goals, and resisted distractions.
* **Visual Heatmaps & Trend Charts**: Track your hourly focus patterns, 7-day focus vs. screen time trends, and most distracting apps.
* **Discipline Levels & XP**: Earn experience points (XP) for every completed session and level up from *New Starter* to *Iron Will*.

#### 5. 🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Guaranteed
* **Zero Cloud Telemetry**: All session data, usage logs, and discipline scores are stored purely in your device's local SQLite database.
* **No Account Required**: No tracking, no data selling, no remote servers.

---

### 🛡️ ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE & PERMISSION DISCLOSURE:
ASAR uses Android's AccessibilityService API and UsageStats API strictly to detect foreground app launches during your active focus sessions and display the blocking intervention. ASR **never** reads keystrokes, personal messages, web content, or sensitive user data.
