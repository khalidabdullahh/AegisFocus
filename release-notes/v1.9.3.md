## Aegis Focus v1.9.3

- Fixed: Strict Mode no longer locks you out of the whole Android Settings app. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, display, sound and battery work normally during a strict session.
- Strict Mode still protects itself: any Settings screen that shows Aegis Focus (Accessibility, Device admin, App info, Apps list) is closed right away, and the uninstall dialog stays blocked until the session ends.
