## Aegis Focus v1.8.0

- New App Limits (Productivity tab): set a daily time limit and/or a maximum number of opens for any app. When the limit is hit, the app is covered by a "limit reached" screen until tomorrow.
- New App PIN lock (Settings): Settings and Productivity ask for a 4 digit PIN, so limits and gates can't be changed on impulse.
- New protection alert: you get a notification if the accessibility service is turned off.
