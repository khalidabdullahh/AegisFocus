## Aegis Focus v1.15.3

- Strict Mode now protects only Aegis Focus itself. Android Settings stays open, and installing or updating any app works during a strict session.
- Still blocked during Strict Mode: uninstalling Aegis Focus, Force stop, Clear data or storage for Aegis Focus, switching off its Accessibility service, and deactivating its Device admin.
- Other apps can be uninstalled as usual. Browsing the Apps list, the Accessibility list and search in Settings works normally.
- On a phone set to a language other than English, the Settings protection stays strict (any screen showing Aegis Focus is closed).
