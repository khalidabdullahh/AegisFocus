## Aegis Focus v1.15.1

- Fixed: a Focus Routine with blocked apps and Strict Mode did not block anything. Routines now get their start alarm as soon as you save, edit or switch them on, and again after the phone restarts or the app opens.
- Fixed: saving a routine while its time window is already open now starts it right away.
- Fixed: a routine started late now ends when its window ends, instead of running for the full length.
- Fixed: overnight routines (for example 11 PM to 7 AM) now use the right weekday after midnight.
- Fixed: if Android refuses the background timer notification, apps are still blocked.
