## Aegis Focus v1.7.0

- New "My Habits" in Goals & Habit Calendar: add your own daily habits, pick the days, tick them off and watch your streak.
- Calendar no longer counts days before you started using the app as missed, and today only counts once finished.
- Fixed the duplicate "1h" focus target button (now 1.5h).
- Settings now always shows the real installed app version.
