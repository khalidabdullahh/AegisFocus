## Aegis Focus v1.15.2

- Fixed: a Focus Routine session that started in the middle of its time window could keep running for hours after the routine ended (for example a 5 AM to 10 PM routine still locked at 10 PM). It now always ends when the routine's time window ends.
- Fixed: a routine session that is already running with the wrong length is corrected as soon as you open the app after this update. If the routine's time is already over, it ends right away.
