## Aegis Focus v1.15.0

- Cloud backup: your habits, Mindful Gates, app limits, YouTube topics, routines and theme are saved to your account (Settings > Cloud backup) and come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. It backs up by itself when you leave the app.
- New "Your week" report in Productivity: focus time per day, best day, habits done and how often Aegis stopped Shorts, YouTube videos and limits.
- Home: tick today's habits right on the Today card.
- Routine templates: Study, Bedtime and Morning in one tap.
- Quick Settings tile: add "Focus 25" to start a 25 minute focus session with one tap.
- Mindful Gate: a 5 second "take a breath" pause before Open.
- Settings is reorganised (Appearance, Security, Cloud backup and Permissions now sit together near the bottom) and screens fade smoothly.
