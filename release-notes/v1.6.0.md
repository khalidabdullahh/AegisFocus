## Aegis Focus v1.6.0

- New Mindful Gate (Productivity tab): pick apps and write your own reminders; the app opens only after you tick every one.
- Fixed Shorts & Reels blocking for YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, and TikTok is now closed when opened.
- 18+ block now also checks the browser address bar, so it still works when browser Secure DNS bypasses the DNS filter.
- New "Blur 18+ visuals" switch in Settings (Android 11+): social and browser apps are scanned on-device and explicit images are hidden behind a blur. Not 100% accurate.
