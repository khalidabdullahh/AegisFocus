## Aegis Focus v1.10.0

- New: **Campfire** tab. Layer calming sounds with a per-sound volume slider for three modes: **Deep Sleep**, **Study** and **Deep Focus**.
- 9 ambient sounds: campfire, rain on tent, brown noise (free) plus night crickets, distant owl, soft wind, far thunder, forest stream and pink noise (Pro).
- 10 ready-made presets, such as Warm Fire, Deep Sleep Night, Exam Focus and Night Coding, and you can save your own mixes.
- Sleep, study and focus timer. The sound fades out gently at the end.
- Keeps playing with the screen off, with Pause and Stop in the notification. It pauses for calls and when headphones are unplugged.
- Each mode has its own background and accent colour. The background moves slowly while sound plays, and the campfire glows in Deep Sleep.
