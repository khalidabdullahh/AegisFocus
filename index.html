Why it exists

Willpower loses to apps built to hook you

Infinite feeds, autoplay and notifications are designed so you never reach a natural stopping point. Reminders and good intentions do not hold up at 11 pm. Aegis Focus works on the environment instead: it makes the distracting thing hard to reach, and makes the focused thing the easy default.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you do not

A focused toolkit in one calm app. No clutter, no fake numbers, no dark patterns.

Strict Mode

Commit to a session and the rules lock. Blocked apps stay blocked, the list cannot be edited and the app cannot be removed until the timer ends. There is no emergency exit, so you pick your duration deliberately.

App blocking

Pick the apps that drain you. When you open one during a focus session, Aegis Focus steps in with a block screen instead of letting you slip through.

Shorts and Reels blocker

Keep the useful parts of YouTube, Instagram and Facebook while cutting the endless short-video feeds. The toggle takes effect instantly, no restart needed.

System-wide ad blocker

One tap turns on a local DNS filter that removes ads and trackers across apps and browsers. It does not send your traffic through anyone's server.

18+ content block

An optional family-safe DNS mode that blocks adult sites in every browser and app. It is a strong first wall, not a perfect one, so pair it with Strict Mode for hard moments.

Clean Streak

A private daily check-in for quitting a bad habit, with a 7-day strip, best streak and a body-and-mind recovery timeline. Nothing about it is shared or uploaded.

Focus sessions

Start a timed session in one tap. Every finished session counts toward your real focus time and your streak.

Recurring routines

Schedule focus for the hours you want protected, for example weekday mornings, and it starts by itself even after a reboot.

Goals and reminders

Set daily and monthly goals, then add reminders at the times that matter. A Productivity tab keeps it all in one place.

Honest usage stats

Screen time and focus time come straight from Android usage data. If there is no data yet, the app says so instead of inventing a score.

Shield tiers

Your shield levels up from Bronze to Diamond as your focus streak grows, and the home tab shows how close the next tier is.

Scam Shield

Clicked a bad link by mistake? Scam Shield blocks known scam, phishing and malware sites before they load, so a movie-site pop-up is less likely to cost you an account.

Clean Streak

Break a bad habit loop, privately

Pornography, endless scrolling, any loop you want out of. Open the Productivity tab and tick one box each day you stay clean. That is all the tracker asks of you.

  • Daily tick. Mark today clean, undo if you mis-tapped, start over without judgement.
  • Streak and best streak. A 7-day strip shows the week at a glance.
  • Recovery timeline. Milestones at 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 90 days describing common changes in body and mind.
  • Stays on your phone. No account, no sync, no one else can see it.

The timeline is general information, not medical advice. If a habit is harming your health, please talk to a professional.

Blocking, explained

Three layers between you and the scroll

Layer 1: apps

An accessibility service notices when a blocked app comes to the front and puts a block screen over it. It runs only when something changes, so battery use stays low.

Layer 2: feeds

The same service recognises Shorts and Reels screens inside otherwise useful apps and steps you back out, so you can still message friends or watch a tutorial.

Layer 3: the web

A local VPN profile swaps your DNS for a filtering one. Ads, trackers, scam and phishing sites and, if you choose, adult domains fail to load in any browser or app. Only DNS lookups are filtered, not your traffic.

Privacy and permissions

Your habits are your business

Focus sessions, goals, reminders, streaks and usage stats are stored on your device. There are no ads and no analytics trackers in the app. An account is only needed if you want to redeem a Pro key, and then only to attach that key to you.

Android asks for some powerful permissions to block apps. Here is exactly what each one is for:

Usage access

Reads which app is open and for how long, to show real screen time and trigger blocking.

Accessibility service

Detects blocked apps and Shorts or Reels screens. It is used only for blocking and nothing is recorded or sent.

Display over other apps

Draws the block screen on top of a distracting app.

Notifications and exact alarms

Delivers your reminders and starts scheduled routines on time.

VPN profile (optional)

Only for the ad blocker. It is a local profile that filters DNS and does not route your traffic to us.

Start after reboot

Restores your reminders and routines when the phone restarts.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is Aegis Focus free?

Yes. Focus sessions, app blocking (3 apps), Strict Mode up to 3 hours, the Shorts and Reels blocker, the ad blocker, goals, reminders and the Clean Streak are free. Pro lifts the limits and adds advanced tools.

Can I get out of Strict Mode early?

No. Strict Mode has no emergency exit by design. While it runs, session rules and blocked apps are locked and the app cannot be uninstalled. Choose a duration you are comfortable committing to.

Does it upload my usage or habits?

No. Your sessions, goals, reminders, usage stats and Clean Streak are stored on your phone. The only server involved is for Pro keys, which checks that a key is valid and unused.

Why does it need Accessibility and VPN permissions?

Accessibility lets the app notice blocked apps and Shorts screens. The VPN permission is optional and only creates a local profile that filters DNS for the ad blocker. See the permission list in the Privacy section above.

Does the 18+ block stop everything?

It blocks a very large set of adult domains in every browser and app, but DNS filtering cannot catch everything, such as content inside some apps or a browser using its own secure DNS. Treat it as a strong first wall and combine it with Strict Mode.

Will it drain my battery?

The blocker reacts to events and sleeps otherwise. The optional ad blocker keeps a small local VPN profile running, which is light but not zero.

How do I get Pro, and can a key be shared?

Tap Get your key in the app and message the owner on WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram. Each key is single-use: once someone redeems it, nobody else can. Redeeming while you already have Pro adds the new time on top.

Is it on the Play Store? Is the APK safe?

It is distributed as an APK for now. The source code is public on GitHub and every APK is built automatically from it by GitHub Actions, so you can inspect what you install.

Which phones are supported?

Any Android phone or tablet on Android 8.0 or newer. Some brands aggressively close background apps, so if reminders or blocking stop, set Aegis Focus to unrestricted battery use.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Message on WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram, or open an issue on GitHub. Feedback shapes what gets built next.
