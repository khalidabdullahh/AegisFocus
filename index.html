Features

Everything you need to stay focused.

Focus Sessions

Set a clear intention, choose your duration, and commit. Circular countdown timer tracks every second.

Real App Blocking

Uses Android's Accessibility API to detect and block distracting apps the moment they open. No workarounds.

Strict Mode

Commit to immutable rules. During Strict Mode, blocked apps and session parameters cannot be changed. Free: up to 3h.

Mindful Pause

Before a blocked app opens, a 5-second breathing moment appears. A prompt asks: why are you opening this?

Discipline Score

A transparent 10–100 score built from your actual behavior: sessions, streaks, block attempts, and exits. No hidden math.

100% On-Device Privacy

No servers. No cloud. No account required. All your data lives in a local SQLite database on your phone — and nowhere else.
